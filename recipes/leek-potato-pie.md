# Leek & Potato Pie

**Serves:** 4 · 
**Oven:** 400°F (200°C) · 
**Bake:** 30 minutes (trial)

## Phase 0 — Defrost pastry

- **30 minutes before prep:** Remove puff pastry from freezer.
- Allow **45 minutes total** to defrost.

## Phase 1 — Prepare ingredients

- 500g potatoes — peel, thinly slice
- 3 large leeks — wash, trim, thinly slice
- 70g butter (trial — up from 60g, used 2026-11-10)
- 225ml double cream
- 100ml vegetable stock, using ½ stock cube
- **1 1/2 tbsp Maille Dijon mustard (trial)**
- **200g Boar's Head Irish cheddar — grate (trial, staged 300g)**
- 1 sheet puff pastry — defrosted
- 1 egg — beat for glazing
- Salt and black pepper

## Phase 2 — Cook & assemble

1. **Preheat oven** to 400°F.
2. **Start leeks first, then potato water.** Melt butter and gently sauté leeks ~10+ minutes without browning — longer is fine, they reduce down nicely.
3. **Make sauce:** Add cream, stock and mustard to leeks. Season with 6 twists salt and plenty of pepper. Simmer and reduce thoroughly while potatoes cook — don't wait for potatoes to finish.
4. **Boil potatoes:** 10 minutes in water seasoned with 8 twists salt, until completely soft. Drain promptly (2026-11-10: held in hot water while sauce caught up — better to start leeks earlier so sauce is ready).
5. **Assemble:** Layer potatoes in baking dish. Cover with 200g grated cheddar (trial), then reduced leek sauce.
6. **Pastry:** Cover filling, crimp edges and brush with beaten egg.
7. **Bake:** 400°F for 30 minutes (trial), ensuring pastry is cooked through, not just browned on top. Rest briefly.

## Notes

- **Potatoes:** Must be fully soft before assembly. Won't soften in oven.
- **Sauce:** Reduce more than previous attempt.
- **Pastry:** 375°F insufficient.
