# Habanero Orange v2

**Serves:** ?

## Ingredients

- 60g Habanero destemmed
- 140g chile yellow peppers cored (ralphs) - substitute sweet peppers
- 1 ruby or blood orange peeled
- 1/2 tbsp cumin
- 1/2 tbsp tumeric
- 1 tbsp paprika
- 1 tbsp pink salt
- 1 cup white vinegar
- Blend until smooth
- CLEAN BLENDER THOROUGLY
- Boil for 1 minute
- Bottle and let sit in the fridge for a few days
- Makes about 11oz (2 standard hot sauce bottles)
