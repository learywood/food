# Chickpea Lentil Chili

**Serves:** ?

## Ingredients

- Red onion, diced
- 3 or 4 garlic cloves, chopped
- 2 carrots, chunky sliced
- 2 Serrano, deseeded, chopped
- 1 tin diced toms (fire roasted preferred)
- 1 tin pinto/cannellini/borlotti
- 1 tin chickpeas
- 1/2 cup red lentils
- 1 1/2 tbsp cumin
- 1 1/2 tbsp paprika
- 1 veg stock cube 24 0z water
- Big pinch oregano (fresh sprig preferred)
- 1 tbsp Mustard seeds
- Pink salt
- Black Pepper
- Sautee garlic, onions. When soft add all spices. Mix well and stir for a minute or so. Then add all other ingredients. Simmer for 30-45, stir often. Will thicken as lentils absorb water.
- Makes 4-5 servings. We have chilli for dinner then blend the leftovers for soup the next day. Add water before blend to get right consistency.
