# Lemon Mojito

**Serves:** 1

## Ingredients

- 1 big squeeze lime juice
- 1/4 less cup Lemon syrup
- 3 shots rum
- Hot sauce dash
- Mint
- Muddle
- Soda water

## Method

1. **Muddle:** mint, lemon syrup, lime juice, and hot sauce in a glass.
2. **Build:** add rum and ice, top with soda water, stir.
